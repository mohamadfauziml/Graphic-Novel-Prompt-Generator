How to turn your story into a video

    

      
1
Generate the script
Copy Prompt 1 into Gemini. Review the script, edit as needed, and copy the final script text.

      
2
Create graphic novel slides
Open NotebookLM, create a notebook, and paste the finished script as a source. In Studio → Slides, open the customization prompt and paste Prompt 2. Choose an available slide size and generate; download the PPTX.

      
3
Turn slides into a video
Upload the PPTX to Google Drive. In Google Vids, use an available Slides-to-video workflow, add narration and review the result. Features depend on your account.
